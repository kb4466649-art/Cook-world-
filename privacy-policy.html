1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use CookWorld. The site is freely accessible without registration.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit CookWorld, certain information is automatically collected by our hosting provider and analytics tools, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
  • Date and time of your visit

Search Queries

When you search for recipes on our site, your search query is sent to the YouTube Data API to retrieve relevant videos. We may log search queries in aggregate (non-personally identifiable) form to improve the site.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Display relevant cooking videos from YouTube
  • Detect your approximate region to show relevant grocery store options
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Analyse traffic patterns to grow and improve the platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies & Tracking

CookWorld uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. We use:

Essential Cookies

These are required for the website to function. They store your language preference and browsing session data.

Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. This data is anonymous and aggregated.

Advertising Cookies

Google AdSense places cookies to serve personalised advertisements based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your selected language and filter preferences between sessions. No personally identifiable information is stored.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising

CookWorld uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense is operated by Google LLC and uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your interests.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ad Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA)
  • Your Online Choices (EU)

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit: Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. YouTube API Usage

CookWorld uses the YouTube Data API v3 provided by Google LLC to search and display publicly available cooking videos. By using CookWorld, you are also subject to:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not store any YouTube video data on our servers. All videos are streamed directly from YouTube's platform. Your interactions with embedded YouTube videos are subject to YouTube's own privacy policy.
